The role

A CTO’s job, taken fractionally: the direction, the delivery, and the handover.

01 · Direction

A roadmap that answers to your P&L

Not a vendor’s pipeline. I sit on your side of the table, decide what deserves to be built, and say no to the rest.

02 · Build

Built by the person you hired

The systems get built by me and the agents I supervise — not handed down to a bench you’ve never met.

03 · Handover

Keys included, always

Code, data, and accounts in your name from day one. If I disappear tomorrow, everything keeps running — and it’s yours.

06 Agents in the workspace Agents that read, write, and file inside Google Workspace — documents, sheets, and drives kept current without a person doing the keeping. Operations

Two ways to engage

Both end the same way: you, owning a system that works.

Mode A

Built with you

We build the system together, in your business, with your people. Your team learns to run and extend it because they helped make it.

  • Your team ships alongside me
  • Capability stays when I leave
Mode B

Built and handed over

I build it, prove it on your real data, and hand it over completely. No black box, no hostage retainer — a retainer exists only if you want the system to keep getting better.

  • Fixed scope, fixed price, real deadline
  • Code, data, and accounts in your name
  • Optional retainer for continuous improvement
